## Enforcement boundary

The checker evaluates tracked and untracked source files under the declared
roots. Generated, dependency, and build-output segments are excluded. A warning
does not fail the gate; a new-file hard limit or legacy emergency ceiling does.
The exception field `max_lines` is retained for metadata compatibility but now
means the measured 2026-08-24 baseline, not an exact no-growth veto. The review
and emergency bands are the smaller of their configured absolute and percentage
allowances. Expiry becomes a validation failure when the program's current wave
exceeds the exception wave.

Existing files below the hard ceiling do not need an exception and may not grow
past the hard ceiling. The ratchet never justifies mixing responsibilities to
stay below a number; domain ownership and reviewability remain the design gate.
