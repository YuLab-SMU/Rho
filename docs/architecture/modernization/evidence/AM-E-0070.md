## Result and review

The tracker now treats severity only as impact and scheduling telemetry. Every
unresolved finding must declare `acceptance_domains`; the closed hard set is
product correctness, data integrity, project isolation, authority/security,
public/serialized contract and recovery truth. An empty set is advisory.

Fixtures prove that an unresolved high-severity advisory finding permits an
otherwise truthful package completion, while an unresolved low-severity hard-
domain finding rejects it. Missing, duplicate and unknown domains fail; old
resolved records remain valid and are reported as `historical`, not silently
reclassified. Two status runs are byte-identical. The real program validates
with 76 findings and 76 work packages; its remaining unresolved high modularity
finding is advisory, while the mixed-generation projection finding remains an
explicit product-correctness/project-isolation blocker for its owning package.
