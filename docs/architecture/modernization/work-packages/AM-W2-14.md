## Boundary

Move exact method groups to `broker_calls.rs`, `guest_calls.rs`,
`contribution_calls.rs` and `runtime_health.rs`, then remove the superseded
`runtime_calls.rs`. Make `release_plugin_admission`,
`complete_plugin_uncertain`, `cancel_plugin_call`, `resume_plugin_error`,
`crash_identity` and `persist_crash_if_needed` `pub(super)` for sibling use. Keep the existing
`invoke_plugin_with_hook` private-parent test seam.

Do not change any method body beyond these visibility qualifiers, nor any call
order, lock/Store access, identity/revision check, grant reservation/completion,
audit, cancellation, guest step, error, schema, JSON, command or recovery rule.
