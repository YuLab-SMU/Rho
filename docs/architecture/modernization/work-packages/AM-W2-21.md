## Boundary

Move Plot/Artifact list and detail projections, clear operations, Plot payload
pruning, retention summary and render run/artifact reconciliation onto one
`ArtifactRepository` over `StoreExecutor`.

Plot/data export continues to hold the Workspace broker lane while it writes a
file, creates the durable artifact record and advances authoritative project
identity. Render execution and interruption remain outside this package.
