## Boundary

Change the internal network and Workspace broker test/runtime seam from
`store_path` to `StoreExecutor`. Convert each exact denied/admitted/failed/stale
or completed event into an awaited `run_store_service` call. Never hold plugin
registry state, a guest store, network response, or Workspace lane guard across
that durable await.

The number, ordering, payload and allow-once consumption flag of audit events
remain unchanged. External network/Workspace work stays between admission and
completion events, and every failure path retains its existing guest cancel,
grant release, route removal and retry behavior.
