## Boundary

Generate `agent_runtime_status` and `agent_runtime_retry`, including their
structured dependency observations. Preserve the exact no-argument calls,
serde fields/defaults, classifications, bounded detail, selected-R identity,
remediation, invalidation event, and the startup fire-and-forget retry that
cannot reject Workspace R or project restore.

Provider/model settings, credentials, network health, package provisioning,
library mutation, Workspace supervision, schema, permissions, and release are
outside scope. Numeric and bundle measurements are engineering telemetry; only
contract drift, nondeterminism, fault-isolation loss, or behavior regression
rejects the slice.
