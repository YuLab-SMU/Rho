## Boundary

Replace every `read_store` use in `commands/plugins.rs` with the application's
shared `StoreExecutor` and execute the existing registry/service method on its
borrowed Store. Generalize only the plugin call families required by those
handlers; preserve owned-Store defaults and exact method bodies.

`runtime_context` captures `project_root` plus the committed Workspace identity
under a short `project_transition_gate` acquisition and never locks
`WorkspaceBrokerLane`. Update, rollback, uninstall and restore already hold the
gate across their full transaction and use an under-gate helper to avoid
relocking. Other handlers do not acquire a new long-lived global gate; their
existing revision/CAS and project-isolation rules remain authoritative.
