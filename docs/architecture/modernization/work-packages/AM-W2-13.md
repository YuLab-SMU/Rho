## Boundary

Move exact helper groups to `recovery_helpers.rs`, `broker_helpers.rs`,
`projection_helpers.rs` and `activation_helpers.rs`. Prefix moved free helpers,
`ReconciliationStatus` and its two methods with `pub(super)` only where sibling
modules require access. Existing `pub(crate)` surface validation helpers retain
their current visibility. `PreparedPluginActivation` remains private inside
the activation-helper module.

Do not change helper logic, arguments/results, public or crate-visible API,
lock/Store ordering, lifecycle, grants, admission, broker authority, errors,
schema, JSON, commands or recovery behavior.
