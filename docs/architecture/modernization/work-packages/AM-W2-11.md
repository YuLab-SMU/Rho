## Boundary

Move `list` and `reconcile_project` to `discovery.rs`. Move
`agent_projection` through `get_panel_contribution` to `contributions.rs` as
additional inherent implementations of `PendingPluginPermissionRegistry`.
Register both private modules from `mod.rs`; external callers continue to use
the same registry methods and facade types.

Do not move or change `request_enable` or later mutation/call methods. Do not
change method visibility/signatures, locks, helper bodies, Store queries,
limits, ordering, errors, schemas, JSON, command registration or behavior.
