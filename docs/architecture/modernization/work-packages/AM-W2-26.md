## Boundary

Execute Surface document rendering, queued Surface events and Check rule packs
through `workspace_plugins::run_store_service`. Clone only owned context/input
into the worker closure, and keep each existing guest invocation plus its
Store-backed validation atomic at the service boundary.

Project transition ordering, Surface queueing/cache publication, guest
fuel/epoch/limits, grant revalidation, Check aggregation and all public DTOs
remain in their current owners.
