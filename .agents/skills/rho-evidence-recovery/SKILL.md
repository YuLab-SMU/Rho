---
name: rho-evidence-recovery
description: Verify Rho analysis results and recover after disconnects, expired observations, partial failure, cancellation, or missing acknowledgements without repeating uncertain actions.
---

Identify the original operation, native target, application receipt, and relevant artifact or file digest. Reports should distinguish what was requested, what was accepted, what the owner confirmed, and what remains unknown.

Use original evidence for claims. Image previews and crops help investigate detail; keep their relation to the original artifact, producing operation, dimensions, and transformation. A rasterized SVG preview is a viewing aid, not a new scientific result. Text and large media have independent continuation and digest checks.

After a connection loss or missing acknowledgement, consult the original receipt and operation record. Accepted scientific work remains owned by the Host. A later step that was never submitted does not become accepted merely because an earlier step succeeded. Reconnection is an opportunity to reconcile, not to replay commands.

A content-change or expired-reference error invalidates the affected chain of pages. Reobserve deliberately and reassess conclusions that combined those pages. Busy observations can retain earlier facts with their original timestamp; they do not establish fresh state.

For partial or uncertain outcomes, describe the evidence gap and the available read paths. Submit new work only after the old request's outcome and the user's remaining intent justify it. Do not claim rollback, completeness, or successful recovery without authoritative evidence.
